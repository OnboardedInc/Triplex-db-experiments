These limitations were not eliminated by green unit tests. The replay proves agreement on its fixture calls and verdicts, not arbitrary-code containment or production acceptance.

Missing from the builder's enumerated limitations: **F11's persistent final-use input deletion/rewrite**. Its report says persistent changes are checked at the end, while these copies are checked only before reuse. The general fallback filesystem warning explains reachability, but does not disclose this specific detection hole; no in-run restore was needed. The stale `workloadIdentity.rule` wording (F12) is also not mentioned. The independent delta test's inherited “gate child” title overstates what it instruments; its actual coverage is reported above.
