471: ### 6.2 The grading rule for a single-arm run
472:
473: 1. **Signals** (R22 section 4.1, pre-registered): host swap growth and pageouts, compressor activity, VM PSI cpu, memory and io, throttled
474:    periods per arm (cpuset regime), neighbor CPU on any arm cpuset, calibration drift (post over pre spin minimum in [0.85, 1.18]),
475:    interference index, hiccup overshoot (driver and observer), driver ELU. Applied per round of 150 s, decided before outcomes are seen,
476:    logged by rule id.
477: 2. **Sequential A/A** (new for single-arm runs): rounds of equal burst phase on the same arm; `d_k = ln(p50_k / p50_{k-1})` of commit
478:    latency; s_d against R22's limits (0.28 at 12 rounds for a 1.25 target) as provisional values until M01's A/A duet calibrates a
479:    sequential limit. Different trace segments enlarge s_d, so this errs toward non-GREEN.
480: 3. **Grade:** GREEN when no round breaks a hard rule, at most one third of rounds are excluded, and s_d is within the limit; AMBER when
481:    only soft rules fire; RED otherwise.
