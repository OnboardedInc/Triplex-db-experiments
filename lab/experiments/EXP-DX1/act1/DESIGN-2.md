# DX-ACT1 · One guard for a page of people

North star P5 requires exact release activation while live reads and commits stay within 1.5×. W0 orders by transaction first, then valid instant, and applies an equal-basis record. ## Three ways to remember progress

**Scope cut.** A closing watermark alone is unsafe. After an unchanged result at 20 is scanned, an old result at 15 can change the item before close. Advancing the watermark then preserves the wrong item. A safe implementation opens a durable intent under a scope fence, stages a new generation, and swaps the visible generation only after roster closure. Every live publisher checks that fence in its transaction. While open, live fact commits can proceed, reconciles must retry or be replayed into the generation under the same fence, and readers see the previous closed generation. Restart resumes the intent and a repeated close recovers its original command receipt. With 9,798 item changes, a sealed immutable roster and 32 changed bodies per transaction, the lower bound is 307 staging commits plus open and close. Roster construction, live catch-up and any additional checkpoints cost extra. This design can delay a live decision for the whole sweep.

**Batched individual guards.** Compare each subject's full durable basis and protect each observed subject, including absence, in the same transaction as its new guard/body. A live winner changes its compare-and-set and the bulk writer rereads on conflict. Readers see the last committed batch. Replay the same command after a lost ACK. At 100k, 32-wide batches need 3,125 commits and 100,000 guard assertions; 256-wide batches need 391 commits and still assert 100,000 guards. A same-scope live save can wait for the entire submitted batch. Measure that wait separately from its own service time.

**Page coverage, selected.** Give each W0 population ID a permanent page and bit by dividing its numeric ID by 256. Other legal subject IDs have disjoint singleton pages. Within each isolated publication namespace the authoritative fact maps disjoint bit masks to exact bases. A clear bit means no decision for that subject. Subsets, recent joiners and newer live decisions keep their own bits and bases.
The selected namespace identifies the page layout on reopen, including for an ordinary live publish without a layout hint. Every live and sweep publisher reads the page and requested item bodies at one position, runs W0 against each member's effective basis, and atomically replaces the page with any changed bodies/pending deliveries. A present page is protected by its unique live fact ID, an absent page by an empty-entity compare-and-set. Every body change replaces the page fence, even at equal basis. A losing writer rereads and recomputes. The page is the only writer of these bodies.
A committed page makes exactly its covered members visible. Uncommitted pages retain their old decisions and may accept an older result until their own page commits. A live change that wins first is included in the retry or supersedes the sweep by basis. A live change that loses rereads the committed page. Readers reconstruct both body and guard at one named position.
A sweep binds each submitted range to its independent ordered roster hash, release, both clocks and exact record bytes. The immutable transaction claim binds the accepted range and original deliveries. Restart replays the same ranges, recovers previous receipts and finishes the suffix. A range claim is keyed by its original first ordinal; different record bytes or a conflicting segmentation are rejected. Completion requires contiguous, nonoverlapping receipts covering the entire roster. No scope-wide watermark advances on partial success.
For 100,000 contiguous subjects, 391 page transactions write 391 page facts plus one body per changed item owner (9,798 in BM1). Unchanged subjects incur no individual write. Each replaced fact also has a retraction. The per-page CAS still permits unrelated pages and live fact writes to proceed. A fragmented roster or a smaller frame can require more page transactions.

## Measurement

Use the real 1k W0 fixture, source-pinned native slot, independent publisher/receiver and new-process readback. Stop at page boundaries, lose the ACK, race earlier and later live records, and include an uncovered neighbor and a partly filled last page. Compare observed masks, bodies, receipts and complete roster coverage.
The fourth server arm uses the standard release with page guards. Keep the three scheduled part-1 arms available and identify the incorrect historical timing separately. Worker trials share frozen inputs and use ordered publication. Reserve CPU for reader/writer roles and measure the 1.5× ratios at each worker count before choosing one.
