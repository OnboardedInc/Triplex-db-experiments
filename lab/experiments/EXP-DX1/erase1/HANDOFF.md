## Required layout and publication behavior

- Keep Datomic for exact facts, history, journal order and durable receipts. Its stored facts and change records carry an opaque protected-value ID and non-sensitive metadata. Keep the protected body and any value-derived sort, lookup or export bytes in authenticated envelopes protected by independently erasable keys. Group independently erasable field key families under each person. Erasing one protected field revokes its family across all versions in one transition. Use independently erasable assertion keys where an individual assertion must be removed. Whole-person erasure revokes the person root. A later authorized assertion uses a fresh key epoch.
- Publish a durable identity-only erasure event and ledger entry before returning completion. A current erasure sequence covers all data bases and survives restore. Every read begun after publication applies that sequence, including cached, pinned, native, journal and explain reads. A reader that cannot verify the current sequence must wait or fail before returning a protected value. Preserve assertion IDs and time intervals with the typed marker. Apply Q36f to the erased SSN and keep pending work keyed by stable effect IDs. [Erasure rulings](../../../acceptance/RULINGS.md) · [Key and ledger design](../../../research/R38-erasure-design.md)
- Require proof that backups, WAL, wrapped keys and live caches cannot recover an erased protected body. Include legacy plaintext copies in migration and restore evidence. Recheck the erasure sequence when publishing a decision or replaying an import. A replay of the erased assertion must retain the marker. A Publish that starts before an erasure must rebuild the affected decisions against the current erasure sequence before emitting them. A newer, authorized assertion is a separate identity.
- **DX-W1.** The commit tail and cache entries carry opaque IDs and an erasure-aware read proof. Authorize unsealing against both the captured data position and the current erasure sequence. Reject or refresh a stale view before a later request uses it.
- **DX-COMPACT1.** The compact row, claim log, journal, outbox and any covering key refer to the same protected version. Preserve the exact old public receipt and the journal's typed replay shape modulo erasure. Every value-bearing copy shares the revocation fate of its protected version.

## Numbers for the next gate

Use a provisional request-to-publication budget of **100 ms p99** for one person and **1 s p99** for a batch of 100. The foreground path should make one batched ledger/key transition with **zero Datomic index jobs in that path**, regardless of retained size. For ordinary saves, require **zero additional synchronous storage turns** and report p50/p99 and added bytes per save.

Pace any native ciphertext cleanup in jobs of at most **2,000 matched datoms** until the measured quiet-account commit, read and feed p99 ratios are each **≤1.25×**. Collect 500 verified saves per control and maintenance block and record how many fall entirely inside the job. Verify zero protected-byte returns from new reads at every tested pin and zero change to bystander values or receipt identities. Repeat at the prepared 16M-fact rung with 415 target edits, at 10 and 100 people, during import and Publish, and after restoring a pre-erasure copy with the current ledger.
