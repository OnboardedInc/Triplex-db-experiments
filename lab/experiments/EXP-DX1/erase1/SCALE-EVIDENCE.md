`smoke-f` is the 24-person functional gate. The earlier `smoke-a` through `smoke-e` attempts preserve the absent image pin, absent old tag, wrong vendor JAR path, Node parameter-property syntax mismatch and interrupted wait for a shared timing window. None entered the measured denominators. Their cleanup receipts cover the private resources each attempt acquired.
