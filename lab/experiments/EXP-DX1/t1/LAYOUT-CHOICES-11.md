# Layout choices priced, not adopted

North star: P2 small-commit latency, while P1/P3 exact pinned reads, ordinary triples and feed recovery remain the floor. The measured corpus is 300 successful primary payloads per current-layout run, 100 per fact band, repeated in a full-level zero/256-reader/zero session. Replays use that current run's immutable peer values after standard layout-3 seeding, not a new-layout seed or a clone of the sealed 100k volume.

All pure apply numbers, size forecasts, host state, controls, input identities and the deliberately limited prediction model are in [REPLAY-11.md](REPLAY-11.md). No new stored layout, migration, reader implementation or default is built. B/C/E retain the one-step logical row delta in this sample; that is not proof about arbitrary queries, historical reconstruction, imports, concurrent transactions or non-strict scopes.

The row-delta check covers the full 17-column row value and add/retire intent. It does not execute the real historical readers or reconstruct retirement coordinates from transaction metadata. The independent oracle passed on today's stored layout only.

The medium band illustrates why datom-only pricing is wrong here. In the loaded full replay (swap 3.994 GiB, replay Mac CPU p50/p99 14.1/144.1%, same-session zero-reader controls in REPLAY-11): A/B/C have 61/37/36 datoms, but 19,582/19,444/19,316 canonical Fressian bytes. B's paired apply gain is .021 ms, C's .077 ms; the median apply drop for C is 14.6%, below 30%. C removes most AVET visits but does not remove the repeated text or its two serializations. E adds three datoms and has no reliable gain.

D lowers medium canonical bytes to 7,278 and pure apply median from .686 to .396 ms. Its predicted 256-window median is 12.085 ms versus A's measured 12.991 ms; the small prediction is 11.237 versus 11.711 ms. These are forecasts under the loaded native CPU p50/p99 15.0/109.1%, swap 3.994 GiB and current full tracing, not measured new-layout commit latencies. They still exceed 10 ms before reader/identity costs. The minimum ID list averages 625.9 UTF-8 bytes/commit in the balanced corpus, before framing/datoms, and would reduce the saving further.

Weeks are rough planning assumptions for readers, compatibility, exactness gates and seed/migration work, not measured implementation durations or promises. The ranking prices milliseconds only and does not authorize a contract/default change. Adopting any new physical layout needs a versioned layout, fresh seeds, old-scope compatibility or migration, the differential, pinned/current/history/feed/duplicate/lost-ACK checks, and crash/restart plus loaded oracle validation. None is claimed here.

At 100k, the 17.50-GiB sealed seed cannot be cloned with only 0.97–1.06 GiB above the live 15-GiB floor. No A/C/E 100k storage-read comparison is claimed. The hypothetical layouts' cache, storage and migration impact at that scale remain open.
