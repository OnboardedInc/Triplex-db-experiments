**Service budget.** `TX2_WRITE_BUDGET_US` prices each indivisible live or bulk write as 2,400 µs per physical turn plus 1,100 µs, 45 µs/prepared op and 70 µs/KiB of request per member. The byte term comes from the matched Mac cycle. The operation term includes provisional validation/preparation work. Over-budget singletons receive `WriteBudgetExceeded` with no write. Ready groups stop before the next member exceeds budget. Bulk frames stand alone and yield. A storage stall or definitive group retry can extend actual waiting.
**Isolation.** In the [budget grid](small/BUDGETS.md), with short tails and bulk ready, 7–8 ms gave 1.128× at 50/s and 1.245× at 100/s, at modeled saturation of 176 and 207/s. At 235/s the short-tail best was 2.876× live-only, or 2.951× with bulk. The saved-tail model missed 1.25 at all three rates and failed to sustain 235/s. Isolation still needs measured service at scale.

**Capacity.** In [matched Mac arms](small/CAPACITY.md), throughput at 1/8/32/64/128 outstanding was 46–49/77–87/138–159/189–195/158–183 saves/s with compiled-request reuse. The control ranges were 35–57/60–66/123–131/146–196/119–172. Largest actual native group was 75. Raising outstanding work above 64 reduced throughput in both reuse repeats.
**First limit.** `TX2_GROUP_REUSE_PREPARED=1` checks initialized layout, installed function and absent journal entities before reusing the validated request. At depth 64, serial repeat preparation dropped from 1.43–1.95 ms/save to about 0.01 ms. One speculative `d/with` per save and the durable round trip remain. Native compute was 0.105–0.168 ms/save. Await includes the log and peer; these samples do not isolate their individual clocks. A conservative reference forecast is 260–350 saves/s at large groups, or 180–215 with the 8 ms budget.
