**Policy.** `TX2_LIVE_PRIORITY=1` enables one writer queue, independently of grouping. The TypeScript runtime or transaction can label `writeClass` as live, import, activation or notice. Unclaimed work defaults to import. Within the live or bulk tier, validated accounts rotate. A shared subject, fact, claim, ordered sender or whole-scope precondition stays behind its predecessor. A maximum of 64 pending bulk requests reserves live admission. Continuous live overload can starve bulk.
**Bound.** `TX2_BULK_ROWS` and `TX2_BULK_BYTES` bound each bulk admission. Each runs alone and the scheduler checks waiting live work before selecting another. `DatomicClient.transactBulkFrames` yields the original durable result per caller-defined atomic frame. A hard elapsed-time guarantee is impossible during a storage stall.
