### Archived p3r

1. Cohort. There were 144 fixed-ID non-oracle, non-straddle misses in the fourteen slow 1,024-seat blocks, with 274 matched HTTP exchanges and 130 repair exchanges. All 274 have request write, headers and body-end marks. 217 headers arrived at the Node callback at least 50 ms after the gateway finished. The mean full miss was 831 ms. Of 729 ms outside the gateway, 624.1 ms was gateway-finish to headers, 38.7 ms between exchanges, 38.2 ms from the Node write callback to native entry, 13.3 ms before first and after last HTTP, 8.0 ms in socket queue and write, and 6.9 ms reading the body. Native admission was 99.5 ms and admitted work was 1.33–2.02 ms per miss.
2. Attribution. Recorded same-isolate work or GC overlapped 258 exchanges and 301 ms per miss in that 624 ms interval. `reader.observe` on other replies overlapped 215 ms across 233 exchanges and GC overlapped 131 ms across 257. Those times can overlap. JSON parse and the named row-decode span overlapped 12.8 and 7.8 ms. The uncovered callback interval can include delivery or scheduling. The persistent HTTP agent allowed 512 sockets per client and each of the six reader workers had at most 171 logical readers. The old route had no separate fallback seat gate. Socket reuse, connect time, head-of-line delay, per-response wire size and pre-read seat wait were unrecorded. Across 84 contained worker windows, the counters record 88.6M decoded rows, 11.17 GB of decoded row text and 128,984 repairs. Only 422 of 129,349 prior-ingestion waits found all needed revisions. Encode, parse and named decode below 0.5 ms can be omitted. A miss's own observation averaged at least 5.08 ms across 108/144 sampled spans. Independent model verification ran afterward.
   Using 831 ms as full miss residence and retaining the fast blocks' other cycle time still predicts about 17k reads/s in the slow blocks. The observed 4.1k/s would require about 215 ms in the other cycle under the fully busy model. Local roots averaged 6.90 ms there versus 4.06 ms in the fast blocks. The trace does not cover the rest of each closed-loop cycle.
3. Weighted and forecast. The four sampled weighted misses used seven exchanges including three repairs. Native wall averaged 3.00 ms, external time 4.45 ms, and the interval from final HTTP to read finish 3.79 ms. Callback delay after gateway finish was near zero. This small sample supports coverage and reuse for weighted reads. A single seat still waits for the isolated worker's decode and IPC. For a scope aged like p3r, forecast closure 192 at 3.9–5.4k weighted and 0.45–0.65 stress, or closure plus isolated install at 4.1–5.9k and 0.52–0.80. The combined range assumes fewer obsolete-cut exchanges. Early-scope smoke rates and an overloaded driver need their own interpretation.

### Diagnostics and handoff

4. Mac curve. Slot ready after 82m44s from queueing, including one refused image and a private rebuild. Two mirrored 2-second arms per condition used 128 closed-loop seats, 24 roots and two repeated old cuts. They checked 7,855,809 contained reads and joined 21,243 native exchanges for 22,083 logical fallbacks including boundary reads. The prior arm uses the public reach path.

At 5%, direct arms averaged 31–90 waiting seats and had 127,040 local completions while misses were pending. The native queue averaged 2.59–57.44 ms and admitted work 0.11–0.47 ms per miss. Even the lower service-only ceiling was 84.8k/s. Install reduced 6,710 HTTP exchanges to 28 and average waiting seats to 6.7–6.8. Repeated cuts make reuse unusually high here.
Packed budget replay followed the curve, using 38,826 frozen offers and synthetic 24 KiB entity replacements. The 64/192/512 MiB settings held 27.7/83.2/193.5 KiB per touched subject across 2,364 touched subjects and zero extra history for 7,636 untouched. Misses at 8 minutes were 1.93/0/0%, at 20 minutes 4.95/0/0%, and at 40 minutes 9.38/5.72/0%. All missed at the one-hour cut outside the clock window. The synthetic latest/last-2,000 mix stayed local in all three.
The first recovery setup failed its retained-cut check before timing. The corrected fixture starts the follower at a sealed current cut and applies the next journal live. The retry waited 3h37m48s across seven admission attempts before ending with another run still holding the lab lease. Total time from queueing to admission or cancellation was 5h00m32s across the curve and the retry. The owned Datomic volume and reservations were released. The 41,392/s recovery comparison remains open.
5. Unit regression. Nineteen local seats and one historical seat used the same exact 4,096-row body in both binaries, with two 700 ms arms per condition. On `89cf9d4d2` the mixed arms completed 1,493/6,441 local reads versus their zero-miss controls (23.2%, below 90%). With Part 2's isolated lane they completed 6,547/6,157 (106.3%). Eleven and ten historical replies respectively verified before the throughput assertion. This isolates inline decode and observation with installation off. The repeat held 94.6%. TypeScript and all 27 focused tests passed. [Regression test](../../../bench/test/rd1-fallback-rate.test.ts)
6. Handoff. The three 115-second offer pairs each have a 28-minute foreground budget and a matched zero arm. The diagnostic phase schedule and shell syntax were checked locally. Full-profile gates and write-gateway allocation attribution remain open. [Server 1 diagnostics](SERVER1.md)
7. Audit. Archived analysis checks source, run, slot and boot on both clocks, then joins each HTTP parent and response ID and verifies the time sums. It hashes the analyzer, report and clock reference. Archived events are explicitly historical. Native comparisons use a fresh scope, owned slot, exact receipt model and exchange census. The packed replay checks the exact selected version and content at every locally covered cut. The unit regression checks the exact response digests and route on each seat in fresh arms, records the fixture and loaded product hashes, and checks the rate relative to the controls.
