# k100 reading: one 100,000-subject north-star pair, gateway reads (2026-11-12)

A reading, not a verdict. Code: DX-R3 head (`b648ac2b` before the history rewrite, `be202d43` after). Seed: the sealed
100,000-subject seed. Reads go through the read gateway (no local replica reads). Guest `nohlt` on, no agent running.

1. **Zero-reader arm: report status DONE.** 7,547 background commands offered, 7,431 committed, 116 expired at the 30 s
   limit. The driver's own bars: 5-10 fact band p50 23.6 ms and p99 142 ms in the quiet L256 window (bars 10 and 50);
   11-25 fact band p50 26.0 ms and p99 318 ms. Steady bands over the whole run: p50 31.6 / 40.2 / 45.6 ms, p99 5.0 to
   5.2 s. Burst bands: p50 about 1.1 s, p99 30 s (the expiry limit).
2. **Ladder arm: report status ERROR, no bar table.** All 17 phases ran. The analysis then refused the zero-reader arm as
   its matched baseline, because that arm has commands that did not commit (`loadBaselineWrites` in `lab/bench/driver.ts`
   requires every baseline command to be committed). 15,556 background commands offered, 10,612 committed, 4,944 expired.
   The sweep phase took 1,274 s.
3. **What the raw records show.** With no readers and about one command a second, a commit takes 18 to 45 ms at the
   median (10,000 subjects: about 9 to 12 ms). In the burst windows the commit path falls behind by seconds even with zero
   readers, so the 10,000-subject burst trace offers more than the commit path takes at this size. With 64 to 1,024 readers
   the quiet-window commit median is 78 to 120 ms.
4. **Cache evidence (a lead, not a proven cause).** The read gateway's entity cache (218 MB budget) held 45,718 entries at
   the end and recorded 5.40 million evictions and 5.45 million misses against 49.8 million hits; its revision cache
   recorded 31.7 million misses against 23.5 million hits. The write gateway's entity cache held 29,425 entries with
   164,987 evictions. The caches are sized for 10,000 subjects.
5. **Not measured:** P1 read latency per level, P3 lag and P4 pulls at 100,000 subjects, because the ladder analysis did
   not complete. The raw per-read samples are not retained; a rerun needs either a baseline rule that tolerates expired
   commands or a trace paced to what the commit path takes at this size.
