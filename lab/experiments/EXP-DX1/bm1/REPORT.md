# DX-BM1 bare-metal bench

At 10,000 subjects, all-commit ACK p99 was **36.11 ms with zero readers and 45.08 ms under load**, against 50 ms. Each arm captured all 38,826 commits. At 256 readers, small and medium commit medians were 6.05 and 7.52 ms against 10 ms.
**The loaded run is ineligible for a performance verdict.** At 1,024 readers all six active reader workers reached 99.8–100% event-loop utilization. The sender averaged 4.9%. Goodput was 0.422 of the 256-reader rate against 0.9. Weighted pulls rose from 2,293/s on the earlier retry to 3,490/s, below 4,400/s.
All 143,900,955 reads matched the model with zero errors. Independent replay matched 351 sampled reads, 18,857 entity queries and the receipts. The Mac copy reproduced those comparisons. The previous 10k retry’s sole error in 89.38 million observations was a socket reset before an answer.
At 256 readers, writer p99 grew to 1.478 times its matched zero-reader value against 1.25. Feed p99 was 26.50 ms against 250 ms. Recovery, maintenance density and the steady writer queue also missed their thresholds.
**A correct 100,000-subject activation still has no measured cost.** Both private clones passed all 13 native guard schedules. The first R1 run stopped on duplicate answer links for subject 6011 at the retained head. The independent W0 oracle accepted its 165 seed facts and rejected its 175 later facts. The earlier 73.78 s omitted durable guards.
A fresh seed takes about 3¼ hours to create and verify before the three guarded arms. That recovery follows SCALE1’s replacement first item. The cleaned bench was handed over at 18:48:15 EST. [Handoff](HANDOFF.md)
The old/new/old zero-reader comparison was 140.54 / 41.70 / 141.61 ms p99. Both Mac night pairs remain unjudged after the reader hit its 8 GiB limit. Four hours stalled in activation plus eight idle server hours cost about $13.08. [Mac night](MAC-NIGHT-2026-11-18.md)
