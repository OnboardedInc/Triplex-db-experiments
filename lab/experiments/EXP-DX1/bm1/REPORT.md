# DX-BM1 bare-metal bench

**The smallest import frame moved 5,491 facts/s, more than triple the forecast.** At 50 background saves/s, the quiet account’s save p99 was 48.58 ms against 21.31 and 10.92 ms in its two controls (2.28× and 4.45×). Its read p99 rose 6.18× and 7.50×. All 32,400 scheduled offers across the five windows verified. Wider frames moved little more and delayed saves much more.
**Deep saves took about 7 ms longer at the median**, with matched work and zero readers. After the first two seconds, p99 was 170–179 ms deep and 19 ms thin. In 49 of the 50 slowest deep saves the largest interval was peer wait outside each save's own evaluation. Activity between replies limits any single uninterrupted write-gateway pause to 29 ms in those 49 cases. The transactor's log lacks individual pause times. All 7,092 receipts joined the exact TAIL1 replay. [Evidence manifest](SHA256SUMS) SCALE1’s 100k run gave 63.23/46.85 ms at 39.4/s in its zero/64-reader arms (2,364 saves each). Its 1–3-field Zipf tape came after 570 seconds of live traffic on a verified restore. BM1 used five asserts and five retirements across distinct subjects for 45 seconds on a freshly seeded 10k scope. Both used one write-gateway core with a 3 GiB heap and unforced GC. SCALE1 gave the reader gateway two cores to BM1’s one. [Comparison](HANDOFF.md#matched-deep-tails)
At 10,000 subjects, all-commit ACK p99 was **36.11 ms with zero readers and 45.08 ms under load**, against 50 ms. Each arm captured all 38,826 commits. At 256 readers, small and medium commit medians were 6.05 and 7.52 ms against 10 ms.
**The loaded run is ineligible for a performance verdict.** Its 52 bar readings include 36 MET, 6 MISS, 10 UNJUDGED. At 1,024 readers all six active reader workers reached 99.8–100% event-loop utilization. The sender averaged 4.9%. Goodput was 0.422 of the 256-reader rate against 0.9. Weighted pulls rose from 2,293/s on the earlier retry to 3,490/s, below 4,400/s.
All 143,900,955 reads matched the model with zero errors. Independent replay matched 351 sampled reads, 18,857 entity queries and the receipts. The Mac copy reproduced those comparisons and recovered the full trace viewer and commit anatomy. The previous 10k retry’s sole error in 89.38 million observations was a socket reset before an answer.
