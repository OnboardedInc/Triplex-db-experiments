Its affinity allowed overlap with the transactor cores. The native ladder used these two JVMs. Synchronous commit, fsync and full-page writes stayed on. WAL sync time is PostgreSQL's cumulative timing divided by sync calls. It includes index work and yields a mean. CPU comes from five-second process counters.
